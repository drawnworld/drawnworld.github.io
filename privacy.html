Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This policy explains how Drawn World ("we") handles data in connection with our website and our private tool "Drawn World Uploader".

Who uses the tool

Drawn World Uploader is used only by the Drawn World team to publish videos to the Drawn World accounts on YouTube and TikTok. It is not offered to the public.

What data the tool accesses

  • With our own permission, the tool connects to our own YouTube and TikTok accounts through their official APIs (Google OAuth and TikTok Login Kit).
  • It receives access tokens and basic account information (such as the account ID and display name) needed to upload videos.
  • It uploads video files, titles, descriptions and hashtags that we created ourselves.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to upload, schedule and manage our own videos.
  • Access tokens are stored locally on our own computer and are never sold, shared or sent to third parties.
  • We do not collect data from viewers or other users, and we do not use cookies or tracking on this website.

Google and TikTok data

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of TikTok data follows the TikTok Developer Terms of Service.

Deleting data

We can revoke the tool's access at any time in our Google and TikTok account settings, and delete the stored tokens from our computer. Questions or requests: drawnworldyt@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
